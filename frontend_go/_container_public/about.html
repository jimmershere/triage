Our story

The HEDI collaboration journey

HEDI started as a skunkworks project between revenue cycle analysts and Golang engineers who were frustrated with slow EDI turnarounds. We began by automating a single 837 claims feed for a Medicare Advantage pilot and quickly expanded into eligibility, status, and remittance flows.

Today HEDI blends Python-powered translation, Go-based orchestration, and a PostgreSQL event backbone to shorten every feedback loop between clearinghouses, payers, and front-line staff.

Milestones that shaped us

  • 2018: Partnered with a CMS ZPIC contractor to harden our first Medicare claims intake gateway.
  • 2020: Introduced RabbitMQ-driven job queues so Trish, our virtual helper, could track every acknowledgement in near real time.
  • 2022: Open-sourced our claim entry prototypes to help state Medicaid agencies modernize their X12 tooling.
  • 2024: Rolled out the portal experience you use today—role aware, analytics friendly, and tuned for rapid onboarding.

Why teams choose HEDI

People-centered workflows

We design every page so operations teams can spot status changes instantly and share context with clinical or finance staff without leaving the portal.

Cloud-ready architecture

Our Go services stream data through PostgreSQL and RabbitMQ, while Python parsers keep pace with CMS and commercial payer updates.

Community roots

From Linux Mint workstations to open specification mapping, we listen to implementers and publish improvements back to the healthcare data community.
